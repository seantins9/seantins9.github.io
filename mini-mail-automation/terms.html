Use information

Mini Mail Automation is an internal tool operated by Sean for the owner’s Gmail account and separately authorized Crisp workspaces. It is not offered for public signup or use by customers.

Authorization and limits

The owner must authorize Gmail access before it begins. Google’s read and send permissions apply to the mailbox. Application code limits reading to configured business-alert work and verification of its own sent reports, and limits sending to the owner-only daily Crisp report described on the application homepage.

Instructions found inside an email do not create permission to act. Gmail authorization does not authorize payments, changes to customer data or entitlements, GPU operations or production deployments. Those actions remain subject to their separate task authorization and review requirements.

Human review and reliability

Operational alerts and generated diagnoses may be incomplete or mistaken. The owner remains responsible for reviewing proposed actions and applying the existing approval and release checks. The application checks its own sent message against the prepared report. This verifies the sent record, not receipt by the recipient.

Stopping use and questions

The owner can stop the automation and revoke Google access. Retention and deletion are described in the privacy notice. Questions can be sent to shenghaowu97@gmail.com.
